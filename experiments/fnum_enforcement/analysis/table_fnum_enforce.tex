\caption{Effect of enforcing the f-number during quality-diversity
discovery. Matched ablation: the only change from the unconstrained pool
(\texttt{enforce\_fnum:false}, where the f-number penalty is gated off and
absent from the objective) is \texttt{enforce\_fnum:true} at penalty weight
$w_{f/\#}$; the lens template, full-DOF parameterization, $(S_2,S_3)$ archive
(bins and bounds), 100k-evaluation CMA-MEGA budget, and seed are identical.
All metrics are over the top-100 designs by fitness after L-BFGS refinement.
On-spec band $f/\#\!\in\![3.6,4.4]$; sharp $\equiv$ RMS $<20\,\mu$m.
$n_{\mathrm{sharp}}$ is the number of top-100 designs under 20\,$\mu$m and
$\epsilon$-dist.\ counts those that are mutually distinct at $\epsilon\!=\!0.30$
in normalized $\theta$ (i.e.\ diverse \emph{and} sharp). Enforcement pins the
aperture but roughly doubles the best achievable RMS and collapses the number
of diverse, sub-20\,$\mu$m designs.}
